# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to discuss simulation, parallel computing, and research results.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks.
- Give constructive, specific feedback and credit others' contributions.
- Respect different levels of experience and communicate professionally.
- Report results honestly, including uncertainty and limitations.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on a relevant comment or discussion to report abuse privately to GitHub. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation in the repository. For security vulnerabilities, use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting option under the repository's **Security** tab if it is available.
